# Ontology

The types this vault uses, and the relations it writes between notes. Nothing
enforces this note. It is what the completion offers and what an agent reads.

## Types

- Note: anything that is not one of the below
- Concept: an idea the vault holds more than one note about
- Source: something written elsewhere, clipped or cited
- Periodic Note: a day, week, month, quarter or year
- Book: a note with a book beside it
- Reference: how something here works

## Relations

- about: this note is about the target
- depends-on: the subject needs the target
- part-of: the subject is a piece of the target
- supports: the subject is evidence for the target
- contradicts: the subject is evidence against the target
- cites: the subject quotes or draws on the target
- answers: the subject answers the question in the target
