# Code of conduct

kasten is one person's notebook, shared in the open. This page says how
people treat each other in its issues, pull requests and reviews.

## What we ask

- Be kind. Assume the other person means well and is doing their best.
- Argue about the code, the docs and the idea, never about the person.
- Say plainly what you mean, and take a plain answer without offence.
- Take a no. The maintainer decides what goes into kasten, and a request
  turned down is not a slight.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, slurs, and mockery of who someone is: their sex, gender, sexual
  orientation, origin, religion, age, body, disability or skill.
- Harassment: sexual remarks, threats, stalking, or contact that goes on
  after someone asked for it to stop.
- Publishing what is private to someone else, such as an address, an
  employer, or the text of their notes.
- Spam, and posts written to start a fight.

## Where this holds

In this repository: issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages and
discussions. It also holds anywhere someone speaks for kasten.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer, [pgoell](https://github.com/pgoell), in private.
Say what happened, where, and add a link or screenshot if you have one. The
maintainer tells nobody who reported.

If your report is about the maintainer, or you get no answer, use
[GitHub's report form](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## What happens then

The maintainer reads every report and answers it. Depending on how bad it
was and whether it happened before, the maintainer:

1. asks the person to stop, in private;
2. edits or removes the post and warns them in public;
3. blocks them from the repository, for a time or for good.

A report made in bad faith is itself a breach of this page.
