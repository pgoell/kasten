# Save path orderings: the log card and the palette's log commands

Part of [save path orderings](/reference/save-path-orderings.md). The columns are explained in [How to read the table](/reference/save-path-orderings.md#how-to-read-the-table). Rows keep their numbers on every page. The format of the lines is [Log format](/reference/log-format.md).

## Orderings

| # | Ordering | Must hold | Loses typed text if wrong | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | A tap on the card, or the palette's entry while today's note is in the focused pane (whether or not its editor holds the focus), with or without a write of the note in the air. | One transaction in the buffer, at the end of `## Log` (made at the end of the note where there is none), isolated in the history so `u` takes back that tap alone. Autosave writes it as it writes typing, naming its base, so rows 1 to 28 hold for it and a vault that moved refuses it with `Changed on disk`. No write of its own. | no | log-card: `a tap appends the default line at the end of ## Log`, `a tap makes ## Log where the note has none`, `two quick taps come back one undo each`, `with vim on, a tap leaves vim in normal mode and u takes it back`; palette: `the drink entries writes today's own note in its buffer as one change, and calls no route`; home-route-log-drink: `the palette logs into today's focused pane when the focus is off its editor` (#620) |
| L2 | Two quick taps on the card. | Both lines land, in the order tapped. | no | log-card: `two quick taps both land, in order` (#620) |
| L3 | The card's `Undo` after the tapped line was edited, moved off its start or taken out by hand. | Nothing is written, and the card offers no `Undo`. Otherwise `Undo` takes out exactly that line. | yes | log-card: `Undo takes the tapped line out`, `no Undo once the tapped line was edited` (#620) |
| L4 | The palette's entry with no pane on today's note: two quick commands, a save refused with `409`, a create racing another, a failed write. | Each command reads today's note, appends the line and saves naming the read as its base through `updateNote`, which reads again and retries on `Changed on disk`. A note the listing lacks is created with the `<leader>gd` template; a create answered `NoteExists` writes into the note there. Commands run one after another, so two land in the order given, and a failed one rejects with its message in the notice and does not hold up the next. While today's note holds text the vault refused, or a save of it is in the air, or it has come into the focused pane, nothing is written and the notice says `Nothing logged:` and why; this is asked at the press and again just before the write. | no | log-write: `a drink logged into the vault reads the note again after a changed-on-disk refusal and lands`, `a drink logged into the vault lands two quick taps in order even when the first save answers late`, `a drink logged into the vault updates the note that is there when the create finds it already made`, `a drink logged into the vault rejects a failed write, and the next tap still lands`, `the palette logs nothing while today's note is being saved`, `a queued drink writes nothing when today's note became unsaved while it waited`; home-route-log-drink: `the palette logs nothing while today's note holds text not yet saved` (#620) |
| L5 | A line written under `## Log`, `## Done`, `## Time` or `## TODOs` (and `## Highlights`) by `appendUnder` or `appendUnderEdit`, into a note whose heading carries trailing spaces or tabs, or whose note holds that heading inside a fenced code block or the frontmatter, or whose section holds a fence with a heading-shaped line in it. | The heading is found by the readers' rule (`log.py`, `log-lines.ts`): the line reads exactly the heading once trailing spaces and tabs go, outside any fence and below the frontmatter. The line lands at the end of that section, after any fence in it, so no second section is made and no line lands where the readers skip it. With no such heading the section is made at the end of the note, after a closing line if the note ends inside a fence nobody closed (row L6); a heading inside such a fence is not used. The section still ends at any heading, where the readers end `## Log` only at `#` or `##`. | no | note-section-headings: `puts the line under ## Log with trailing spaces, not under a second one` and the same for `## Done`, `## Time`, `## TODOs`; `skips a ## Log inside a fence and puts the line under the real one` and the same for the other three; `makes ## Log at the end when the only one is inside a fence` and the same for the other three; `does not end the section at a heading inside a fence in it`; `puts the line under ## Log with a trailing tab`; `a ## Log only in an unclosed fence gets the fence closed and one live ## Log` (#755); `skips a ## Log in the frontmatter`; `a line put under a fenced ## Log is counted by drinkTotals` (#746) |
| L6 | A line written by `appendUnder` or `appendUnderEdit` into a section that runs to the end of a note ending inside a fence nobody closed, or a note with no live heading that ends so. | The closer is the opener's own run (three backticks, four, or tildes), placed after the note's last line, so the line goes after it. Nothing is removed, and blank lines at the end of the fence stay inside it. Two writes leave one closer. A fence in a later section is left alone. Inline triple-backtick code opens no fence and gets no closer. A fence in a list item gets no column-0 closer, and neither does a fence under a lazy continuation line of one (`- a`, `b`, blank, indented fence) or under a marker with five or more spaces after it. An indented top-level fence still gets one. A fence-shaped line in the frontmatter opens nothing. In the editor the closer and the line are one change, so one `u` (vim on) or undo (vim off) takes both back. The card's Undo button takes back the line and leaves the closer. | no. Without the closer the line sits where the readers never count it. | note-section-headings: `a line into a ## Log ending in an unclosed fence lands after a closer`, `a line into a ## Log ending in an unclosed fence is counted by drinkTotals`, `a note ending in an unclosed fence with no newline gets the closer on a line of its own`, `the closer repeats a four-backtick opener`, `a tilde fence is closed by tildes`, `two lines into a ## Log ending in an unclosed fence leave one closer`, `an unclosed fence after the section is left alone`, `blank lines at the end of an unclosed fence stay inside it`, `a line of inline triple-backtick code opens no fence`, `a note ending in inline triple-backtick code gets no closer`, `a backtick line that opens no fence gets the real fence closed`, `a fence in a list item gets no column-zero closer`, `a fence under a lazy list line gets no column-zero closer`, `a fence under a wide list marker gets no column-zero closer`, `an indented top-level fence still gets a closer`, `a body below frontmatter ending in an unclosed fence gets a closer`, `a fence-shaped line in the frontmatter gets no closer`, `drinkTotals counts a line written after a backtick line that opens no fence`; note-section: `an embed section ends at the next heading after a line of inline triple-backtick code`; log-card: `with vim on, a tap into a ## Log ending in an unclosed fence closes it and u takes both back`, `with vim off, a tap into a ## Log ending in an unclosed fence closes it and undo takes both back` (#755); log-lines: `a line of inline triple-backtick code hides no log line` (#758) |
| L7 | A food logged from the card's search, by `Log this food` while the panel is open. | One transaction at the end of `## Log`, as L1 writes a drink, with the panel closed in the same change. Its undo is isolated, so `u` or the card's `Undo` takes that line alone. Only `Log this food` writes: a search, a barcode read, `Back` and `Escape` write nothing. | no | log-card: `#621 Log writes one food line at the end of ## Log and the card totals count it`, `#621 with vim on, keys typed in the search field never reach vim and u takes the food line back`, `#621 with vim off, undo takes a food line back and the card's Undo too`, `#621 grams of zero, negative or text write nothing and say why`, `#621 Escape closes the search without writing`; palette: `#621 the palette's Log food opens the food search on a daily note` |
| L8 | A food search answer, or a barcode read, that arrives after the panel closed, after the note changed, or after a later search began. | The answer is dropped: its search is no longer the latest, or its panel is no longer on the page, so nothing is drawn from it. | no | log-card: `#621 a slower earlier search answer never replaces a later one` |
| L9 | A tap on a kind the config note names (a kind of your own, or a default kind with its own tap amount), with vim on or off. | As L1: one transaction at the end of `## Log`, isolated in the history, so `u`, undo or the card's `Undo` takes it back. | no | log-card-kinds: `#623 a tea line in the config note puts a tea button on the card and its tap writes - 🍵 1 cup`, `#623 with vim on, a tea tap leaves vim in normal mode and u takes it back`, `#623 with vim off, undo takes a tea tap back`, `#623 the card's Undo takes a tea tap back`, `#623 a config line can change water's tap to 500 ml` |
| L10 | The config note answers after the card was drawn, fails, or is missing, and a tap comes before it answers. | Until it answers, the card draws the default kinds and a tap writes their line. A missing or unreadable note gives the defaults. Nothing is ever written to `log.md`. | no | log-card-kinds: `#623 with no config note the card shows the three default kinds as before`, `#623 a config note that fails to load gives the default kinds` |
| L11 | A line added from the log pane, on today or another day, while that day's note is closed, open in another pane, open to type in the focused pane, holds text the vault refused, or is being saved. | The write goes through the note's write queue and `updateNote` (read, append under `## Log` by L5 and L6, save naming the read as its base, read again on `Changed on disk`). While the note holds refused text, is open to type in, or is being saved, nothing is written and the notice says `Nothing logged:` and why; this is asked at the press, in the queue and inside the change. A note open in another pane gets the written text in its query, as after a todo pane write. | no | use-log-actions: `#624 a log write is refused while the note holds unsaved text, is open to type in or is being saved`, `#624 a log write puts the written text in the note's query and asks the log again` (#624) |
| L12 | A line added from the log pane on a day whose daily note does not exist, or is made by another write in the meantime. | The note is made with the template `periodicNote` gives (the one `<leader>gd` uses) and the line under `## Log`. A create answered `NoteExists` writes into the note there instead, so no second note is made. | no | use-log-actions: `#624 addLine on a past day with no note creates it from the daily template with the line under ## Log`, `#624 addLine on a past day finds the note made meanwhile and writes into it` (#624) |
| L13 | An edit or a delete of an entry from the log pane, after the note changed since the pane read it, or with a save refused with `409`. | The line is found by its number and its exact text. If that line no longer reads so, nothing is written and the pane says why. A refused save reads again and retries through `updateNote`, so a change made meanwhile elsewhere in the note is kept. The guards of L11 hold. An edit typed as no amount writes nothing. | yes | log-edit: `#624 replaceLine patches the one line and keeps the rest byte for byte`, `#624 an edit or delete of a line that changed since the read writes nothing and says why`, `#624 an edit typed as no amount writes nothing and says why`; use-log-actions: `#624 editLine reads again after Changed on disk and lands` (#624) |
| L14 | The pane's `Undo` after a delete, when the note changed where the line was. | The line goes back at its number only while the line above it still reads as it did at the delete; otherwise nothing is written and the pane says why. One delete is kept for undo. | yes | use-log-actions: `#624 undoDelete puts the deleted line back where it was and refuses when the note changed there` (#624) |
| L15 | `Save` in the food search's add form, with `foods.md` missing, changed since the read, or failing to save. | One line goes to the end of `foods.md` through `updateNote`, which reads again and retries on `Changed on disk`, so a line added meanwhile elsewhere is kept. A missing note is created with the line. A failed save keeps the form and says why, and nothing is logged. The daily note is not touched until `Log this food`, which is L7. | no | my-foods: `#799 adding appends the line at the end of the note`, `#799 adding to a missing note makes it with the line`; log-card-my-foods: `#799 Add food saves the food with the query as its name and opens it in the serving form`, `#799 a failed save says why and keeps the form` (#799) |
| L16 | The palette's `Log <name>` for a kind the config note names (a kind of your own, or a default kind with its own tap), with today's note in the focused pane or not, with vim on or off. | The entry writes that kind's tap line, never the default kinds' line. In the focused pane it is L1: one isolated transaction, so `u` or undo takes it back. Elsewhere it is L4: the same queue, refusals and reads again, with the written line in the notice. | no | home-route-log-drink: `#770 with log.md the palette offers exactly its kinds and Log tea writes - 🍵 1 cup into today's focused note`, `#770 Log tea from another note writes - 🍵 1 cup into today's note through the vault`, `#770 a configured water tap of 500 ml writes - 💧 500 ml from the palette and the notice names it`, `#770 on a phone, the toolbar's run button opens the palette and Log tea writes into today's note`; palette: `#770 with vim on, the palette's Log tea over today's note leaves normal mode and u takes it back`, `#770 with vim off, undo takes the palette's Log tea back`; log-write: `#770 a tea tap through the vault waits its turn and a refusal writes nothing` (#770) |
| L17 | The palette opened before the config note answers, after it was written or deleted elsewhere, or when it fails to load. | Until the note answers, while it is missing, and when its first read fails, the palette offers the three default kinds and writes their lines. A later read that fails keeps the last kinds. A write to the note reaches the entries without a reload; a delete puts them back on the defaults. A broken line gives no entry. The route reads the note under its own query key, so a conflicted log.md closed and opened again is still read off the vault. Nothing is ever written to `log.md`. | no | home-route-log-drink: `#770 without log.md the palette offers Log water, coffee and alcohol and Log water writes - 💧 250 ml`, `#770 the palette's entries follow a write to log.md without a reload`, `#770 a removed log.md puts the palette back on the default kinds`, `#770 a log.md that fails to load gives the default palette`, `#770 a broken line in log.md gives no palette entry and the other kinds still do`, `#770 a failed refetch of log.md keeps its kinds in the palette`, `#770 a conflicted log.md kept and closed is read off the vault again when opened` (#770) |
