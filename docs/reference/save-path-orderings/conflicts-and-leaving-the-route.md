# Save path orderings: live conflicts and leaving the route

Part of [save path orderings](/reference/save-path-orderings.md). The columns are explained in [How to read the table](/reference/save-path-orderings.md#how-to-read-the-table). Rows keep their numbers on every page, so "As 82" points to the same row wherever it sits.

## Orderings

| # | Ordering | Must hold | Loses typed text if wrong | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 92 | Type T1; somebody else writes X; W1 refused; somebody else writes T1. | `reconcile` says yes to a conflicted note with nothing in the air, and the refetch's `allowReload` takes T1: the conflict clears, status saved, and T1's held entry goes. No `:w` or `:e!`. | no | `clears a live conflict once another writer writes the buffer's text`, `drops the held entry of a live conflict that cleared itself`, home-route: `clears a live conflict by itself once the vault comes to hold the buffer's text` (all red on main) (#269) |
| 93 | As 92, but somebody else writes Y. | `allowReload` takes nothing: the conflict and the buffer stay, and the bar's diff reads Y. | yes | `keeps a live conflict and the buffer when another writer writes other text`, home-route: `shows the diff against the newest vault text when it changes again during a live conflict` (#269) |
| 94 | As 92; type T2. | W2 names the vault's T1 as its base and lands. | no | `writes the next key on the vault's text once a live conflict cleared itself` (red on main) (#269) |
| 95 | As 92 up to the refusal; `:w` out; somebody else writes T1. | The event is declined and the note marked missed, then reloaded once `:w` answers. | yes | `declines and marks missed the buffer's text written while :w of a conflict is in the air` (#269) |
| 96 | A route chunk is gone after a release; the page reloads. | A gone script reloads through the router while it renders, the editor still mounted, so as 29 to 31 text not yet sent is held at `pagehide` and is back on screen after the reload. A gone stylesheet reloads through kasten's error screen, which happens only entering `/`, so no editor is unmounting. | yes | e2e `frontend/e2e/release.ts` S4 (script) and S6 (stylesheet), `chunk-reload.test.tsx`, and the tests of rows 29 to 31 (#379) |
| 97 | Type T1; leave the editor route inside the quiet period, so the flush W1 goes out; close or reload the tab, or a phone backgrounds the app, before W1 answers. | T1 is held under its base before W1 goes out: the route's `pagehide` listener went with the route, and the page cancels W1 unheard. The next opening offers T1. | yes | `holds the flush on leaving the route when the page goes before it answers`, `holds the flush on leaving the route when a phone backgrounds the app before it answers` (both red on main) (#382) |
| 98 | W1 out, nothing waiting; leave the route; the page goes before W1 answers. | T1 held; nothing more sent. | yes | `holds a quiet write in the air on leaving the route with nothing waiting` (red on main) (#382) |
| 99 | W1 out; type T2; leave the route; the page reloads, a chunk reload as in 96 too. | T2 held beside its base, naming T1, and offered on reopening as an error, not a conflict. | yes | `offers the flush on leaving the route after a reload that cancelled it (#381)` (red on main) (#382) |
| 100 | As 97 or 98, but the page lives: the write lands, or fails. | Landing drops the held copy. A failure holds the text once, with the vault's reason. A later hide holds nothing. Leaving one note for another within the route holds nothing at the leave: the hide does that, and holding at the leave would list each note left on the bar until its flush lands. | yes | `drops the text held on leaving the route once the flush lands`, `drops the text held on leaving the route once the quiet write in the air lands`, `keeps one entry when the flush on leaving the route fails`, `holds nothing on leaving the route once every write answered` (#382) |
| 101 | Type T1; leave the route; come back to the note before W1 answers, the editor on the cache, which holds T1. | The new editor takes T1's held copy as the text on screen and does not drop it: the cache holds T1 because the leave put it there. Typed nothing, then the page dies: T1 stays held. W1 landing drops it. Typed T2, then the page dies: the buffer follows the leave flush's queue, so T2 is held under the vault's base with T1 beside it, and reopens as an error, not a conflict (#386). | yes | `keeps the flush held when the route comes back on it before it answers` (red before this fix), `drops the flush held when it lands after the route came back on it`, `never lands the flush over text typed after the route came back` (#382, #386) |
| 102 | Type T1; leave the route; come back before W1 answers; W1 fails. | The buffer that came back reads the failure: status error, and T1's held entry is taken as the text on screen, not listed beside it, once. The next key names the vault's text, with no false refusal. | yes | `takes the leave flush's failure into the buffer that came back on it` (red on main) (#387) |
| 103 | As 102, but `:e!` before W1 answers. | `:e!` queues the write of the chosen text behind W1, as in 76, so the vault and the screen end on the chosen text and T1 never stands over it. W1 failing holds nothing. Refused, with the write of the chosen text failing too, the next key names the chosen text. | no | `writes the text :e! chose behind the leave flush after the route came back`, `holds nothing when the leave flush fails after :e! once the route came back` (both red on main), `names the text :e! chose after the route came back when the leave flush is refused` (#387) |
| 104 | As 102, but type T2 after coming back; the page dies, or a phone backgrounds the app, and W1 never lands. | T2 is held under the vault's base with T1 named beside it, and reopens as an error, not a conflict. | yes | `never lands the flush over text typed after the route came back` (red on main), `holds text typed after the route came back as an error when a phone backgrounds the page and it dies` (#386) |
| 105 | As 102, but the page lives: type T2 after coming back; W1 lands or is refused. | T2 goes out behind W1, naming its answer. A refusal holds T2 as a conflict, once: its entry carries T1, and nothing goes over the other writer's text. `:e` without the bang, a rename or a delete wait for W1 as in 68 and 72, and another note's queue stays its own. | yes | `names the leave flush's answer for text typed after the route came back`, `holds both texts when the leave flush is refused after the route came back and text was typed`, `refuses :e after the route came back while the leave flush is in the air`, `lets a rename after the route came back wait for the leave flush, and refuses when it fails`, `keeps another note's queue apart after the route came back` (all red on main), `keeps text typed after the route came back when the note is reopened within the route on the leave flush's text` (#387) |
| 106 | As 55, but W1 answers before the quiet period ends, so the queue has drained when W2 goes out. | The reopened buffer follows W1, so W1's answer is its base: W2 names W1's stamped text and lands, no conflict. W1 failing leaves the vault on the base, which W2 names and lands over; T1 stays held, as in 56, since T2 was typed over it. | no | `names the flush's answer when it lands in the quiet period after a reopen on its text` (red on main), `names the old base when the flush fails in the quiet period after a reopen on its text` (#389) |
