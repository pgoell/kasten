# Save path orderings: the log card's weight kind

Part of [save path orderings](/reference/save-path-orderings.md). The columns are explained in [How to read the table](/reference/save-path-orderings.md#how-to-read-the-table). Rows keep their numbers on every page. The rows before these are in [the log card](/reference/save-path-orderings/log-card.md). The format of the lines is [Log format](/reference/log-format.md).

## Orderings

| # | Ordering | Must hold | Loses typed text if wrong | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L21 | A weight written from the card's `⚖ Weight` button, with a number in the amount field. | One transaction at the end of `## Log`, as L1 writes a drink, isolated in the history. The amount field is emptied. The card's Undo, `u` (vim on) or undo (vim off) takes the line back. | no | log-card-weight: `#805 A4 the Weight button writes the typed number as a weight line, and Undo takes it back`, `#805 I3 with vim on, u takes back a weight line written after an ask`, `#805 I3 with vim off, undo takes a weight line written after an ask back` (#805) |
| L22 | `⚖ Weight` with the amount field empty, or Enter in the field while the card asks. | Nothing is written. The card asks for the number: the why line says `Type the weight in kg`, the amount field takes the focus, and its placeholder says `weight in kg`. Enter while the card asks writes the line, as pressing the button does. | no | log-card-weight: `#805 A5 with an empty amount the Weight button asks for the number`, `#805 A5 Enter in the amount field while asking writes the weight line` (#805) |
| L23 | A typed amount that is no weight, such as `abc`, `0` or `82.4 lb`, with or without a question on the card. | Nothing is written. The why line says why, in the warn colour, and the amount field keeps its text. The question stays, so a fixed number and Enter write the line. A write or an Undo of the card ends the question, and an edit elsewhere in the note does not. A config that drops the kind ends it too. The typed amount survives the card's other writes, such as a food log or a copy of yesterday, because the field is remembered across the redraw. The log pane asks the same way: Enter writes after an ask, a refused amount keeps the ask, and a change of day ends it. A comma decimal such as `82,4` and a typed unit such as `82.4 kg` write. | no | log-card-weight: `#805 I1 an amount that is no weight says why, writes nothing and keeps the text`, `#805 I1 a comma decimal and a typed unit both write the weight line`, `#805 I1 after a refused amount, a fixed number and Enter still write the weight line`, `#805 I3 the question's words and placeholder clear on the next write, and a refused amount keeps its text`, `#805 I3 an edit elsewhere in the note while the card asks keeps the typed number and the focus`, `#805 I3 a line added under ## Log while the card asks keeps the typed number, the focus and the question`, `#805 I3 a config that drops the weight kind ends the ask` (#805) |
| L24 | The palette's `Log weight`, over a daily note's card or anywhere else. | Over a card it asks on the card, as L22, and writes nothing. Anywhere else it opens today's note, which waits for its card and then asks there as L22, and writes nothing. | no | palette-weight: `#805 I4 Log weight over a daily note's card asks on the card and writes no line`, `#805 I4 Log weight away from a card hands the ask to today's note and writes no line`; home-route-log-food: `#805 I4 Log weight away from a card opens today's note and asks on its card` (#805) |
| L25 | A second weigh-in on the same day, then the card's Undo. | The day's value is its last line, so the card shows the later value, and after Undo the earlier one again. | no | log-card-weight: `#805 I2 a second weigh-in shows the later value, and Undo shows the earlier one again` (#805) |
| L26 | The read of the 30 days before the note's day, which the card makes whenever the config has a kind that keeps its last value, whether or not the day has a value of its own. | One read per view and day. A failed read is no earlier value, and the console warns. A config with no such kind asks for nothing more than yesterday. The day's own value shows first, and the 30-day answer only fills in a day with none. | no | log-card-weight: `#805 A6 with no weigh-in today the Weight button shows the latest one before the day, with its date`, `#805 A6 with no weigh-in in the 30 days the Weight button says so, and the read is asked once per view and day`, `#805 A6 a failed read of the 30 days is no earlier value and warns`, `#805 A6 the 30-day read is asked only where a config has a kind that keeps its last value` (#805) |
