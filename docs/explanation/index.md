# Explanation

Why kasten is built the way it is. These pages are for reading away from the
keyboard; they answer "why" rather than "how".

* [The vault and the derived index](vault-and-derived-index.md): why the notes live on disk, and Postgres holds nothing a note needs
* [Finance in Postgres](finance-in-postgres.md): why money is the one record kasten keeps outside the vault, why Postgres and not a ledger file or SQLite, why agents may read single transactions, and what backing up the database now means
* [Security model](security-model.md): why kasten has no login of its own, what the gate covers, the two prefixes left open, what the shell and the chat pane can reach, and the consent and page-fetch checks the backend makes itself
* [Two environments](environments.md): why the maintainer's dev and prod are deployed in deliberately different ways, the constraints that box imposes on both, why the shell and the agent logins in it must sit behind the gate, why Claude Code is installed on first start and not in the image, and why prod images are built for amd64 and arm64
* [File and folder limits](file-size-limit.md): why an authored file stays under 5,000 tokens, why a folder holds about seven entries, why the checks count bytes and keep a baseline, and why a file or folder over a limit is split along a deep module
* [Code quality checks](code-quality-checks.md): what each check beyond the file size limit catches, why its limit sits where it does, why the frontend's list of exempt files only shrinks, why comments are held short, why a person still reviews one area at a time, and why the review of each pull request runs in CI
* [Deleting a note](deleting-a-note.md): why a delete moves the note into a hidden folder instead of removing it
* [Moving a note](moving-notes.md): why a move rewrites links before it renames, why a drop asks and the prompt does not, why index entries move only when they were written, why a folder kasten makes gets an index, and why the backfill waits to be asked
* [Live preview and the vim mode](live-preview.md): why the editor renders markdown in normal mode and shows you the source in insert mode, and why an embed is a read-only note that listens to its source and stops three deep
* [kasten on a phone](kasten-on-a-phone.md): why vim is off under a finger, why a split stacks its panes, why a tap raises no keyboard, why the toolbar stays above the keyboard, why a todo takes two taps and has no Undo, why kasten installs as one app, why it opens with no signal, what an open tab does across a release, and what no real phone has confirmed
* [The workspace](the-workspace.md): why each browser and phone reopens the panes, splits and tabs it had open, why that lives in the browser and not the vault, why a phone and a laptop keep apart by pointer, what is kept, and what a gone note, a link and a broken save do
* [The archive](the-archive.md): why finished work goes in a folder rather than a field, and why one key rather than a filter on every list
* [The file beside a note](books-in-the-vault.md): why what you read is its note's path with the suffix swapped, why the suffix is one of two, the two doors a file comes in by, why a highlight is found again by searching the page for its words, and why the vault's history never takes a copy
* [OKF in the vault](okf-in-the-vault.md): why the vault is an Open Knowledge Format bundle, what the one adopted field buys, why the backfill is not a save and when to turn it off, what a note renamed onto a reserved name costs, and why the vocabulary is a note nothing validates
* [Spaced repetition](spaced-repetition.md): why a card's schedule lives in the note, why the format was borrowed rather than invented, why a deck is a tag rather than a note, why a deck nests, why the review has two front ends, and why a daily limit counts off the schedule
* [People in the vault](people-in-the-vault.md): why a person is a note with a type rather than a record, why their card counts links and never names, what that costs the vault as it stands, and why nothing was indexed to build it
* [The graph](the-graph.md): why the graph is read off the vault on every request, why its query is parsed on the backend when todos are parsed in the browser, why the graph reads aliases the editor does not yet, and why the language is two shapes rather than Cypher
* [The agent boundary](the-agent-boundary.md): why a token reaches ten capabilities and the finance reads under a prefix of their own rather than the routes the browser uses, what it cannot do, what a leaked token exposes, why the write is conditional in one direction only, why the consent step checks its own origin, and what one Caddy block is holding
* [Finance reads past the boundary](the-agent-boundary/finance-reads.md): why a token reads the finance summaries and single bookings, why nothing writes, and what a leaked token now exposes
* [Skills read past the boundary](the-agent-boundary/skills.md): why a token reads the Claude Code skills and never writes them, what a skill file may be, and what a leaked token reads of them
* [The todo board](the-todo-board.md): why a todo stays where it was typed and belongs to a project by a `#p/x` tag, its note's folder or a link on its line, why the backend works the project out, why the board opens on lanes by kind, and why it is a mode of the todo pane
* [Shared lists](shared-lists.md): why the shared lists are plain todo lines in two notes, why a second gate and a `/share` prefix stand in front of them rather than a second user, what the second person can reach, and how a conflict, a live update, an offline change and Clear behave
* [The chat pane](the-chat-pane.md): why it runs on the Claude Agent SDK in the shell container, why it has a proxy route of its own, why it speaks HTTP and server-sent events, why there is one session that outlives the page, why tools run unasked, and why the SDK is installed on first start
* [YouTube transcripts without captions](models-on-the-vps/youtube-transcripts-without-captions.md): how MacWhisper transcribes a video that has no captions, what kasten would need to do the same (the download, the model and where it runs, time and money per hour, YouTube's terms, the licenses), and what to build
* [Food from Open Food Facts](food-from-open-food-facts.md): why the food data comes from Open Food Facts, why the backend asks for it, what leaves the server, why kasten counts its own calls, why the cache lives in memory, why search goes to Search-a-licious, and why foods of your own live in a vault note
* [Semantic search models](models-on-the-vps/semantic-search-models.md): which embedding model gives kasten search by meaning and "notes like this one", local on the VPS's CPU or a paid API, what each costs in RAM, time or money for the live vault and how each reads German; pgoell picked local bge-m3 and a hybrid search with BM25, and the page says what kasten would build and why pg_textsearch is the BM25 extension (#954)
* [bge-m3 and pgvector on the VPS](models-on-the-vps/bge-m3-and-pgvector-on-the-vps.md): how fast bge-m3 embeds a query and the vault on the VPS's CPU, measured at four piece lengths, and what pgvector needs on the shared postgres
* [The pieces index](models-on-the-vps/the-pieces-index.md): how the index of the vault's note pieces follows the notes, why a save embeds only what changed, and what the indexer does when something fails
