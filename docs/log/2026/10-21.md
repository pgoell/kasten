# 2026-10-21

* **Update**: [Save path orderings](/reference/save-path-orderings/log-card.md) gained row L5: a line written under `## Log`, `## Done`, `## Time`, `## TODOs` or `## Highlights` finds its heading by the readers' rule, so a heading with trailing spaces takes the line rather than a second section, and one inside a fenced code block or the frontmatter is skipped (#746).
* **Fix**: a capture finds `## Dump` by the readers' rule, the same one as row L5. A `## Dump` in a fence or the frontmatter is skipped, and a fenced heading-shaped line no longer ends the dump. A note that ends inside an unclosed fence holding `## Dump` takes the capture under it (#749). New page [Save path orderings: the dump capture](/reference/save-path-orderings/dump-capture.md): rows D1 and D2.
* **Fix**: a capture never lands inside a fence and never leaves one open (#752). A note that ends inside an unclosed fence gets a closing line after its last line, and the capture goes after that. A capture that opens a fence gets a closer. A `## Dump` that sits only in an unclosed fence is no longer used: the fence is closed and a live `## Dump` is made at the end. Rows D2 to D4 of [Save path orderings: the dump capture](/reference/save-path-orderings/dump-capture.md) now cover this.
* **Fix**: a line written under a heading by `appendUnder` or `appendUnderEdit` never lands inside a fence. A note that ends inside an unclosed fence gets a closing line after its last line, and the line goes after that. A heading that sits only in an unclosed fence is no longer used: the fence is closed and a live heading is made at the end. In the editor the closer and the line are one change, so one `u` takes both back. The card's totals read inline triple-backtick code as no fence too, so the lines after it count. Rows L5 and L6 of [Save path orderings: the log card](/reference/save-path-orderings/log-card.md) now cover this (#755, #758).
* **Feat**: two routes search [Open Food Facts](/reference/http-api/food.md) for a food by name, or read one by its barcode, and answer its nutrients per 100 g and per serving. Answers are cached, and the calls to Open Food Facts stay inside its rate limits (#621).
* **Feat**: the daily note's log card gets a `🍽` button, and the palette a `Log food` entry, that search a food by name or barcode, show recent foods first, take grams or servings and a meal that defaults by the hour, and write one `🍽` line under `## Log`; the card adds up kcal and macros ([Log format](/reference/log-format.md), #621).
* **Docs**: new page [Food from Open Food Facts](/explanation/food-from-open-food-facts.md): why the food data comes from Open Food Facts, why the backend asks, what leaves the server, the limits, the cache and the refusals (#621).
* **Feat**: the food search gets a `Scan` button that reads a barcode with the phone camera (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E) and opens the product in the serving form. The browser's `BarcodeDetector` reads where it exists; elsewhere, such as iOS, the zxing reader loads on the first scan. Nothing but the digits leaves the phone (#622).
* **Docs**: new page [Log food from a phone](/how-to/log-food-from-a-phone.md), and the camera in [kasten on a phone](/explanation/kasten-on-a-phone.md) (#622).
* **Feat**: the daily note's log card reads its kinds and daily goals from `99 Misc/01 Config/log.md`. A line such as `- 🍵 tea: 1 cup, min 3` adds a tea button, and a floor or ceiling shows on the card with a bar, a ✓ or a ⚠. Without the note the card shows the three default kinds as before. Broken lines are named on the card and skipped ([Log format](/reference/log-format.md#the-config-note), #623).
* **Feat**: `GET /api/log` reads `99 Misc/01 Config/log.md` too and answers each kind of your own, its entries and its day's total; without the note it answers as before (#769).
* **Feat**: a log pane, opened by `<leader>gf`, the palette's `Open the log` or the phone's `log` button. It shows a day's totals against the goals of `log.md` and its entries, food grouped by meal; adds, edits and deletes entries on any day, making the day's note where there is none; and draws each kind over 7, 30 or 90 days ([Use the log pane](/how-to/use-the-log-pane.md), #624). Rows L11 to L14 of [Save path orderings: the log card](/reference/save-path-orderings/log-card.md) cover its writes.
* **Fix**: in the todo pane and the board, Enter or Space on a focused button other than the cursor's own row or card presses that button. It no longer opens the cursor row's note or starts the leader (#773).
* **Feat**: the log pane redraws Today and Trends when a daily note changes outside it: a tap on the note's card, a typed line, a write from another device. Events for other notes leave it alone (#774).
* **Feat**: foods of your own. A line in `99 Misc/01 Config/foods.md` such as `- ja! Choco Chips: 30 g, 🏷 4337185123456, 🔥 486 🥩 6.4 🍞 61 🧈 23` (figures per 100 g) lists first in the food search, marked `mine`, and its barcode scans without asking Open Food Facts. The search's `Add food` form saves one, taking the query as the name and an unknown barcode as the barcode ([Add a food of your own](/how-to/add-a-food-of-your-own.md), [the foods note](/reference/log-format.md#the-foods-note), #799). Row L15 of [Save path orderings: the log card](/reference/save-path-orderings/log-card.md) covers the save.
