# 2026-10-31

* **Search**: the ranked search takes a vector hit only nearer than cosine distance 0.5, so a query the vault does not answer, such as `Hund` on the dev vault, lists no notes and the finder on `<leader>fm` says "no notes match" instead of twenty faint rows. BM25 hits stay whatever their distance, and `GET /api/similar` takes no floor (#1341). The measurement is in [The pieces index](/explanation/models-on-the-vps/the-pieces-index.md#how-the-ranked-search-reads-it).
