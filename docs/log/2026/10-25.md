The todo pane and the board draw the assignee as a chip that opens the person note ([The line](/reference/todo-format/the-line.md), [State changes](/reference/todo-format/state-changes.md), #809).

* **Fix**: a wait's or an assignee's name stops at the field after it, in the editor, the todo pane and the backend alike, so on `👤 2026-10-25 Ada ⏲ 2h more` the wait is on `Ada` and leaving `[w]` keeps `⏲ 2h more`, where before the name ran on to `more` and leaving `[w]` dropped it. A `👤` with a field straight after it, `👤 ⏲ 2h Bob`, names nobody and stays words ([The line](/reference/todo-format/the-line.md), #1027).
* **Fix**: a one-word or free-text value stops at a field cut before it, in the editor, the todo pane and the backend alike, so on `a 🔁 every week 📅 2026-10-20 more` the words are `a more`, where before they were `a`, and on `a ⛔ 📅 2026-10-20 more` the `⛔` stays words as `⛔ more`, where before they were `a`. A recurring todo's next copy keeps such words too ([The line](/reference/todo-format/the-line.md), #1030).
* **Feat**: a `finance` schema in Postgres, made by migration `0001`, the first migration kasten has. It is empty until the finance module's tables land, and it is the one exception to the rule that Postgres holds only what rebuilds from the vault ([Finance in Postgres](/explanation/finance-in-postgres.md), [Add a database migration](/how-to/add-a-database-migration.md), #1031).
* **Feat**: finance accounts, balances, bookings and their splits, in the `finance` schema from migration `0002`, and the routes jobs on the host write them through: an account is upserted by its name, a booking is skipped when its account already holds its source key, the splits of a booking must add up to its amount, and each batch pairs the two halves of a move between own accounts into one transfer group. The backend still starts without a database, and the finance routes then answer `503`; the self-host stack runs that way ([HTTP API: finance](/reference/http-api/finance.md), [Configuration](/reference/configuration.md#kasten_database_url), [Self-host kasten](/how-to/self-host-kasten.md), #1032).
* **Feat**: finance bookings are sorted into categories. Migration `0003` adds categories, payees and rules; each booking gets a payee from its cleaned counterparty name, and a category from the first of the hand, a text rule on the purpose, the payee's default and a payee rule, the longest pattern winning. The rules run over history whenever they change and over each new batch, and a category set by hand is never overwritten. The finance pane on `<leader>gk` shows what is still to sort, one key per category and `a` for "always for this payee" ([HTTP API: finance sorting](/reference/http-api/finance-sorting.md), [Sort finance transactions](/how-to/sort-finance-transactions.md), #1035).
* **Feat**: the bank sync, a job in the new `jobs/` folder that pulls DKB and PSD over FinTS (ported from the Firefly importer, with its fix for a CAMT fetch resumed after a TAN) and writes bookings, raw records and balances through the finance routes. It ships in the backend image as `kasten-bank-sync` and runs as a one-shot container on `web`, its logins and FinTS state in `~/.config/kasten/` on the server. Each account's window starts two weeks before its last good one ended, or at its newest booking on a first pull. Migration `0004` and five routes under `/api/finance/sync` record "pull now", let one run start at a time and report the last good pull per account, the last run and the days since a success. A failed run exits `1` for systemd's `OnFailure=`. The source key keeps Firefly's `external_id` as its prefix and adds an occurrence index ([Run the bank sync](/how-to/run-the-bank-sync.md), [Bank sync](/reference/bank-sync.md), [HTTP API: finance sync](/reference/http-api/finance-sync.md), #1034).
