* **Feat**: the bank sync, a job in the new `jobs/` folder that pulls DKB and PSD over FinTS (ported from the Firefly importer, with its fix for a CAMT fetch resumed after a TAN) and writes bookings, raw records and balances through the finance routes. It ships in the backend image as `kasten-bank-sync` and runs as a one-shot container on `web`, its logins and FinTS state in `~/.config/kasten/` on the server. Migration `0004` and five routes under `/api/finance/sync` record "pull now", let one run start at a time and report the last good pull per account, the last run and the days since a success. The source key keeps Firefly's `external_id` as its prefix and adds an occurrence index ([Run the bank sync](/how-to/run-the-bank-sync.md), [Bank sync](/reference/bank-sync.md), [HTTP API: finance sync](/reference/http-api/finance-sync.md), #1034).

* **Feat**: finance plans and goals. Migration `0008` adds plans (a payee or text, an amount give or take, a schedule of every N months or once, an account and a category), the bookings that met each plan's months, saving goals and the leftovers moved into them. Bookings meet plans as they come in and when a plan changes. A fixed category with a plan counts what it cost plus its plans still to come in place of the average; a cost every 2 months or more takes its monthly share off free to spend into a reserve, and its bill comes out of the reserve. A goal reserves part of an own saving account's latest balance, no more than the balance, and its monthly contribution comes off free to spend until it moves into the account. A plan counts from the month it is confirmed in, so one made from history leaves past months alone; a reserve sets aside what its next bill still lacks over the months left, and a bill past it takes the shortfall off free to spend; a bill up to 20 days early or late in the next or last month meets a date its own month left open. Assigning a goal's contribution happens once a month and can be undone, and part of what the limits left over can be moved into a goal. Fourteen routes list and write plans and goals, the month's dates with their matches, a 12-month forecast per scope, and plans proposed from Firefly's bills and recurring payees, which the owner confirms ([Budget model](/reference/budget-model.md), [HTTP API: finance plans and goals](/reference/http-api/finance-plans.md), [HTTP API: finance budget](/reference/http-api/finance-budget.md), #1037).
