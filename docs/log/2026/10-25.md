* **Feat**: the log pane gets its redesign, with Today and Trends side by side on a desktop and as two tabs on a phone. Today has a row per kind and for kcal, protein, carbs and fat, with its total, a meter and the goal in words (`at most 2,200, within`, `at least 120, met`), the log card's own quick buttons and `+ Food`, and the entries by meal. The food search opens in the same sheet as on the card: from the bottom on a phone, as a panel at the pane's right edge over a dimmed pane on a desktop; the inline panel is gone (#996). Trends opens on a summary (average kcal over the logged days, days within the kcal goal as `21 of 27`, the streak), then a small chart per kind and macro in one style, a dashed goal and bars past a ceiling in the warning colour, the macro split as shares of kcal, and a table by week. Weight has empty places in Today, the summary, the charts and the table, which #805 fills. The lines the pane writes are as before ([Use the log pane](/how-to/use-the-log-pane.md), [HTTP API: log](/reference/http-api/log.md), [Save path orderings: the log pane](/reference/save-path-orderings/log-pane.md), #804, #996).
* **Feat**: weight in the log, as a kind that keeps its last value. A kind line of `log.md` may end in `, last` in place of a tap amount, `- ⚖ weight: kg, last`, and the day's value of that kind is its last line, `- ⚖ 82.4 kg`, not the sum ([Log format](/reference/log-format.md), #805).
* **Feat**: an overview pane, `Space g a`, of eight cards: Food, Today, Weight, Water, Flashcards, Todos, Writing and This year, each a big number, a line of words and a small chart, all read from the notes. The log pane's charts and the overview share one set of chart pieces (#806).
* **Feat**: a year review at the top of the yearly note and a week review at the top of the weekly note, drawn above your own text and never written into the file, with GitHub activity when a token is set ([Read the year and week review](/how-to/read-the-year-and-week-review.md), [GitHub settings](/reference/github-settings.md), [HTTP API: notes](/reference/http-api/notes.md#get-apiwrittennotes), [HTTP API: capture](/reference/http-api/capture.md#get-apigithub), #807).
* **Feat**: `👤 [[name]]` without a date names who a todo or a shared list item is for, in any state; the dated `👤 <date> [[name]]` stays the wait, and a line may carry both. Leaving `[w]` drops only the dated one. The todo pane and the board draw the assignee as a chip that opens the person note ([The line](/reference/todo-format/the-line.md), [State changes](/reference/todo-format/state-changes.md), #809).

* **Fix**: after `Space s w`, or `i` on a waiting row in the todo pane, the cursor lands after the wait's own `👤` and date, not after an earlier `👤` that `⏲`, `⏱`, `🆔` or `⛔` holds as its value and the date after it, as in `⏲ 👤 2026-10-25` (#1021).
