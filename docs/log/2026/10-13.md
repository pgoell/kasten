* **Update**: `b` in the todo pane says that a todo waits for another. The list becomes the picker, Enter writes `⛔` and the blocker's id on the todo and stamps a `🆔` on a blocker that has none, and an open todo becomes `[b]`. A row now says what it waits for, `⛔` and the blocker's words, and a blocker says how many wait for it, `→ 2`. On a touch screen `blocked by` in a row's strip does the same. Deleting the last `⛔` from a `[b]` line with the pane's `i` opens the line.
