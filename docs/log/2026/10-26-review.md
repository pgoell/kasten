# 2026-10-26: the review's reads

* **Fix**: the review's daily limits read goes again on a `502`, `503`, `504` or a network error, for about a minute, instead of taking the failure as no limits, so the overview and a sitting no longer count past `new_per_day` while the backend restarts; a read that still fails says so on the overview, in the sitting's foot and in the today pane's Due column, and starts no sitting. A missing `decks.md` still means no per-deck limits (#1149). The parked list says `Reading the notes…` until the card scan answers and shows the scan's error when it fails, instead of `Nothing parked.` (#1150); its reads of the parked notes go again the same way, and a read that answers takes down the alert of one that failed (#1151). The overview names each deck note it could not read, at the foot of the decks (#1153).
* **Fix**: a sitting's read of its deck's notes goes again on a `502`, `503`, `504` or a network error, on the same backoff as the parked list, and one that still fails says `The note could not be read:` in the foot instead of `could not be written` (#1158). The parked list words a put-back whose write fails as `The note could not be written:`, apart from its read alert (#1159).
* **Fix**: when one note of the parked list's or a sitting's read fails for good, the other notes stop retrying before their next try, where before they went on for a minute (#1162). A sitting whose deck read failed for good offers `Try again` under the alert, which reads the deck again; before, nothing read it again until the sitting was reopened (#1163).
