# 2026-10-10

* **Update**: kasten installs to a phone's home screen as one app, where only the capture page did. The manifest at `/manifest.webmanifest` now has `id`, `start_url` and `scope` `/`, the name kasten and icons drawn from the favicon. It keeps the `share_target`, which still opens `/capture`, and gains a `shortcuts` entry for the capture page. `index.html` gained `theme-color` and the `mobile-web-app-capable` and `apple-mobile-web-app-*` metas, and its Apple touch icon is the app's. One app and not two, because a second app with scope `/capture` inside one with scope `/` blocks its neighbour's install on Android and shares its links with it. The `id` changed, so a capture app installed from an earlier release never becomes this one: uninstall it and install kasten. Added [Install kasten on your phone](/how-to/install-on-your-phone.md), which holds the install steps [Capture from your phone](/how-to/capture-from-your-phone.md) used to.
* **Update**: the page's viewport meta gained `interactive-widget=resizes-content`, so the layout shrinks when an on-screen keyboard opens and nothing sits under the keyboard. A new hook, `useViewport` in `frontend/src/lib/use-viewport.ts`, answers whether the window is narrower than 768px and whether the primary pointer is coarse. No component reads the hook yet, so no page of these docs changes.
* **Update**: a window narrower than 768px draws the file tree as a drawer over the page, closed to begin with and closed again by anything that hands the focus to a pane, and draws the focused pane of a tab alone, the splits coming back when the window widens. The tab strip scrolls sideways at any width and keeps the tab on screen in view. [The file tree](/reference/editor-keys/file-tree.md#the-file-tree) and [Panes and tabs](/reference/editor-keys/panes-and-tabs.md#panes-and-tabs) say so.
* **Update**: under a coarse pointer, which is a touch screen, tree rows, the tree's two buttons, tabs, the rows of the palette, the finder, the search, the note prompt and the terminal prompt, and the two buttons of the move prompt are 44px tall, and so is the status bar. Under a mouse every size is what it was. Below 768px the status bar drops the weekday, the week and the version, cuts a long notice short, says `pane 1/2` on a tab holding more than one pane, and is out of reach under an open drawer; the finder and the search give the list the whole panel and draw no preview. [Panes and tabs](/reference/editor-keys/panes-and-tabs.md#panes-and-tabs) and [The note finder](/reference/editor-keys/finding.md#the-note-finder) say so.
* **Update**: a click or a tap on a notice in the status bar opens it out in full above the bar, and one on the save sign of a failed write or a conflict opens the reason and the way out, neither being readable on a touch screen before. Below 768px a notice takes the clock's place, and the finder and the search read no note for a preview they do not draw. Under a coarse pointer an overlay's input is 16px, which keeps Safari on a phone from zooming the page, and the row it sits in is 44px. [Panes and tabs](/reference/editor-keys/panes-and-tabs.md#panes-and-tabs) says so.
* **Update**: the editor starts without vim where the primary pointer is a finger, and `Toggle vim keys` in the command palette turns vim on or off by hand, stored in `localStorage` as `kasten.vim`. Without vim, live preview shows the source of the cursor's line, the line numbers count from the top, a tap follows a wikilink, and Ctrl+Shift+P opens the palette. The palette gained rows for what only vim's keys reached: the write, the two rereads, the three copies, `gf` and the ten tab jumps. The four formatting chords are bound without vim as well. [Editor keys](/reference/editor-keys.md#vim-on-or-off) gained a section and its palette, wikilink and saving sections follow; [Live preview and the vim mode](/explanation/live-preview.md) says why a plain editor reads as insert mode.
* **Update**: [Editor keys](/reference/editor-keys.md) says what each pane answers by touch: the todo pane's state button and `+`, the graph's pan, pinch, tap and larger touch target, the book's edge taps, swipe and `Contents` and `Take` buttons, the image pane's pinch and drag, and the video count that steps on a tap. Three changes reach a mouse as well: `n` or a second click on Jot puts the review's jot line away unwritten, a click on the dimmed page closes a book's contents, and a click on the video count goes to the next video. The image pane no longer reads "no zoom".

* **Update**: a phone, which is a window narrower than 768px under a coarse pointer, draws a toolbar along its bottom edge: `files`, `find`, `search`, `run`, `today`, `todos`, and `pane` on a split, each running the command its leader key runs. The explorer rail is gone there. Every panel over the page closes on a tap or a click outside it. The whole frame makes room for an on-screen keyboard that covers the page, so the cursor's line, the status bar and the toolbar stay above it, and the prompts, the palette, the finder and the search stop at the top of the keyboard; the terminal pane no longer pads for itself. The editor takes the focus after a key and not after a tap, so a tap that opens a note raises no keyboard. [Editor keys](/reference/editor-keys.md) gained "On a phone", which also lists what is still keys only on a touch screen.

[Write a practice exam](/how-to/write-a-practice-exam.md) says `timer` offers five lengths by touch.

The todo pane's `edit` button does nothing while another row is being edited.
