# 2026-10-27

* **Fix**: an Anki import refuses, with a 409 that names the notes and writes nothing, when a card it would add already sits in another note, as it does once a deck is spread over several notes or its note was moved. Before, the import merged into the deck's own note and added those cards to it a second time. A note under the deck's tag with only hand-written cards, and the archive, do not count (#1138). [Import an Anki deck](/how-to/import-an-anki-deck.md) and [the cards API](/reference/http-api/cards.md) say so.
* **Feature**: the video pane has a `Full screen` button in its header, and `f`, which make the player box full screen with the shield inside it, so a click still plays or pauses and the pane's keys still work. Esc, `f` again and an `Exit full screen` button on the player leave it. Where a browser cannot make a box full screen, as on an iPhone, the button is not drawn (#914). [The video pane](/reference/editor-keys/video-pane.md#full-screen) and [touch](/reference/editor-keys/touch.md) say so.
* **Feature**: a YouTube video drawn in a note keeps playing, or stays paused, when it scrolls out of view and back, with the same player throughout; the cursor on its line hides it without stopping it. The note's player now keeps its place in the note's `watching:` field when you pause, as the pane does, so either opens where the other stopped, and a video played to its end drops its entry. Every player takes the speed and captions this browser last used, kept in `localStorage` under `kasten.video.1` (#953). [The three blocks](/explanation/live-preview/the-three-blocks.md) and [the video pane](/reference/editor-keys/video-pane.md) say so.
* **Feature**: live preview and Read mode draw an inline `<svg>` block as its picture, rebuilt from an allow list of elements and attributes before it draws. [Math and diagrams](/reference/math-and-diagrams.md#inline-svg) says what is kept (#952).
* **Fix**: the video pane no longer writes the position of a video its player moved on to, such as a suggestion picked on YouTube's end screen, into the note's `watching:` entry for the linked video; it writes nothing for that video, on a pause, at its end or on close. The pane and the note's own player now read the video the player names through one shared check (#1188). [The video pane](/reference/editor-keys/video-pane.md#where-you-got-to) says so.
