# 2026-10-25: the shared lists

* **Feat**: the lists app at liste.pgoell.com in the Kasten tokens, light and dark with the phone, with a switch between Einkauf and Todo that shows the open counts. Each item has a round tick, its words and a chip for who it is for: "Wer?" when nobody, "Ich" for you, the name for the other person. A tap on the chip opens a menu of the people and Niemand, and a pick writes `👤 [[Name]]` on the line or takes it off. Alle shows every item, Meine yours and the ones nobody has taken. Erledigt sits below, folded to the latest three, with "abgehakt von Alicia, Mo 18:04" under each, read from the jj history. The Eintrag field and Hinzufügen sit at the bottom, above the keyboard on a phone ([Shared lists](/explanation/shared-lists.md), [HTTP API: shared lists](/reference/http-api/shared-lists.md), #810).
* **Feat**: a write through the lists app names its person in the vault's history, `agent(share:Alicia)`, from the address the lists host's sign-in passes, mapped to a name by `KASTEN_SHARE_PEOPLE`; an address it does not map writes as `share`, as before ([Configuration](/reference/configuration.md), #725).
