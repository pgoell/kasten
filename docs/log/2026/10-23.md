# 2026-10-23

* **Feat**: the local graph of the redesign sits at the top of a note's right margin: a small radial graph with the note in the middle and its links around it, each dot in the colour of its first tag, then Links, Backlinks and Unlinked mentions, each with its count. A hover names a dot, a click opens its note, and a switch reaches one step or two. Below 1320 px of pane width the margin goes and a Graph button beside the breadcrumb, or `<leader>gl`, opens the same graph and lists as a 540 px sheet over the note, which Esc or a click outside closes; on a phone it is the bottom sheet. Nothing of the graph is asked or drawn until it is on screen. In a pane whose margin shows, `<leader>gl` still opens the graph pane beside the note. The side sheet slides on a new `--k-slide` token of 240 ms ([Reading a note](/reference/reading-a-note.md), [Panes and tabs](/reference/editor-keys/panes-and-tabs.md#the-graph-pane), [Design tokens](/reference/design-tokens.md), #781).
* **Feat**: the vault graph (`<leader>gg`) groups its notes. In Folders mode each top folder, and each project under a Projects folder, gets a spot that pulls its notes, with a soft round area and its name in small italic serif; in Tags mode each tag gets an area in its colour and a note with several tags sits between them. Dots take their first tag's colour, as in the margin, with a thin ring for each further tag. The layout is Fruchterman-Reingold with gravity in force-graph's own simulation, the same on every open. A 300 px panel holds the counts, the Folders / Tags switch (`m`), the tags with their counts (a click hides one) and a card for the note under the pointer; on a phone, and in a pane under 640 px, it sits under the canvas. The mode is kept per device in `kasten.graph.mode`; the areas use a new `--k-area` token, 8 % in light and 11 % in dark ([The graph](/explanation/the-graph.md#places-and-tags), [Panes and tabs](/reference/editor-keys/panes-and-tabs.md#the-vault-graph), [Design tokens](/reference/design-tokens.md), #782).
* **Feat**: content search (`<leader>fg`) is a page of its own in the redesign: a big serif query field, a 220 px column of filters with counts (folders, tags, the archive), a histogram of matching lines per ISO week for the last 14 weeks, and the results grouped by note with the term in the accent. A click on a bar narrows the results to that week, which the URL keeps as `?week=2026-W38`. Each hit of `GET /api/search` now carries the note's `date` and `tags` ([Finding](/reference/editor-keys/finding.md), [HTTP API](/reference/http-api/finding.md#get-apisearch), #783).
* **Fix**: in the graph pane, Space on any focused button (a note in a pattern's table, `fit`, `depth`, Create, Cancel, as well as the panel's) presses it, as Enter does, and starts no leader sequence; Space on the pane or the canvas still does ([Panes and tabs](/reference/editor-keys/panes-and-tabs.md#the-graph-pane), #855).
* **Feat**: Space shows the which-key panel at once, at the foot of the focused pane: the keys pressed so far on top, then the leader in six columns, Find, Create, Go to, Tabs, Panes and This note, each row the key in mono and what it does. The next key narrows it in place to the keys that letter begins, a click or tap on a row runs it, and Esc, Space again or a key that matches nothing closes it. On a phone it is the bottom sheet, opened by `space` on the toolbar. `?` outside a text field opens key help with its filter focused, and the filter narrows both of its columns, After Space and Vim and the rest ([The leader](/reference/editor-keys/leader.md), #784).
* **Feat**: an HTML page from the vault reaches the leader from inside its frame. Kasten adds a small page script at the end of the page that sends Space, `?` and Escape to Kasten, so Space opens the which-key panel and `?` key help over a page, and Escape gives the keys back to Kasten; in a text field of the page the keys stay the page's. `j` and `k` scroll the page. Kasten acts only on a message from the pane's own frame while it holds the focus, and answers the page's `{kasten: 'hello'}` with the theme and palette. The pane's header reads `PAGE`, the path in mono and "Open in a browser tab" ([The file tree](/reference/editor-keys/file-tree.md), [HTTP API](/reference/http-api/capture.md#get-apihtmlpath), #785).
* **Feat**: an HTML page can opt in to kasten's theme with `<meta name="kasten-theme" content="tokens">`. For such a page kasten puts the token file and the Reader fonts into the page, and writes `data-theme` and `data-palette` on `<html>` from the pane's address; a switch reaches the page by message, with no reload. The page script now sits at the start of the page, so a page's own `var parent` no longer cuts it off, and the page cannot keep Space, `?` or Escape from kasten. A file opened outside kasten keeps its own fallback layer. `GET /api/kasten-theme.css` serves the token file ([HTML theme contract](/reference/html-theme-contract.md), [Design tokens](/reference/design-tokens.md), #786).
* **Feat**: a passage selected in an HTML page from the vault can be taken into today's dump, the way a book's passage is taken. Selecting text shows a Take button over the selection, above it with a mouse and below it on a touch screen, and `y` takes the passage too, in the page or in the pane. The passage goes in as a quote of up to 2000 characters, escaped, under a link back to the heading and anchor it sits in, `[[page.html#anchor|heading]]`, and a link with an anchor opens its page there. A note at the foot of the pane offers Undo, which takes the line out of the daily note while the note is unchanged, and Put it in a note, which writes the passage under `## Highlights`. The page keeps a mark on the passage while it is open. The page script reports the selection and `y` to Kasten, and a hostile page with the focus can forge both, which the page's own quote then shows with Undo ([The file tree](/reference/editor-keys/file-tree.md#the-file-tree), [Capturing into today's dump](/reference/editor-keys/capture.md#capturing-into-todays-dump), [HTTP API: capture](/reference/http-api/capture.md#get-apihtmlpath), [HTML theme contract](/reference/html-theme-contract.md#messages), [Links](/reference/editor-keys/links.md), [A page of HTML runs as nobody](/explanation/security-model.md#a-page-of-html-runs-as-nobody), #787).
