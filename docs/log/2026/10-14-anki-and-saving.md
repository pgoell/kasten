# 2026-10-14: Anki and saving

* **Update**: [Import an Anki deck](/how-to/import-an-anki-deck.md) gained a section on how a note becomes cards: each card Anki made is one line, its question the front template filled in and its answer what the back template adds, so "Basic (and reversed card)" gives two cards and a field lands on the side its template puts it. Conditional sections follow Anki, each card goes to the deck its own Anki card sits in, and a card whose back adds nothing is written as a parked `question::`. "What does not survive" lost the first-two-fields limit and gained the filters, special fields and tags a template shows that the import does not; a card whose question was only a picture or a sound is left out and counted among the dropped media (#231). [HTTP API](/reference/http-api.md#post-apianki) says the same, counts `dropped_media` per card, and answers 400 to a collection with no note types.
* **Update**: [Import an Anki deck](/how-to/import-an-anki-deck.md) says an imported question holding `::`, such as `What does std::move do?`, is written over three lines, question, `?` and answer, with a blank line either side, because on one line the first `::` cut it short. It is left out when its answer is empty or `?`, or when a side is a heading, a line of only tags, a line opening with `!suspended`, or an answer holding a whole schedule comment, any of which would end the card. The overview counts such a card once, as the sitting does (#240). [HTTP API](/reference/http-api.md#post-apianki) says the same, that `cards` in the answer counts the cards the notes hold rather than those Anki made, and that an export whose `collection.anki21b` is not zstd, whose deck has no name or whose note fields are not text answers 400 saying it is unreadable, where it answered 500 (#239).
* **Update**: [Import an Anki deck](/how-to/import-an-anki-deck.md) gained a section on images and sounds: they now land in a `<deck> media` folder beside the deck's note and the card links them as `![](path)`, so a card whose question is only a sound is a card; "What does not survive" keeps only the files the vault will not take (#232). It also says a question ending in `:` is written with a space before the `::`, and a side opening with three backticks gets a backslash (#243). [HTTP API](/reference/http-api.md#post-apianki) says the same, replaces `dropped_media` with `media`, the count of files written, and says a deck name that is not text answers 400 (#244). Its asset sections list `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.ogg` and `.wav` and the bytes each must start with.
* **Update**: [Editor keys](/reference/editor-keys.md#the-review-pane) lists `r`, which replays the sounds of the side showing, and the Replay button that does the same on a phone. The review plays a question's sounds when the card shows and an answer's on reveal, one after the other; the editor draws `![](x.mp3)` as an audio player, and hides the backslash of an escape off the cursor's line (#232, #243).
* **Update**: [Flashcard format](/reference/flashcard-format.md#as-a-cloze) gained the cloze: a line holding `{{c1::text}}` or `{{c1::text::hint}}` is one card per number, asked with that number's text hidden as `[...]` or `[hint]`, and its Back Extra is the `?` form with the cloze directly above the `?`. A cloze keeps one schedule comment per number, `<!--SR:c1!2026-08-20,4,270-->`. [HTTP API](/reference/http-api.md#get-apicards) says the card scan finds that comment on a line of its own (#233).
* **Update**: [Import an Anki deck](/how-to/import-an-anki-deck.md) says a cloze note arrives as a cloze, marks and hints as Anki had them, and with its Back Extra as the `?` back, where before `{{c1::Paris}}` became `==Paris==` and nothing asked it. A Back Extra that would end the card is dropped and the cloze kept, a note of any other type still turns marks into highlights, and so does a mark kasten does not read, one holding a brace or no text, and the reported count takes a cloze as one card per number. "What does not survive" lost cloze. [HTTP API](/reference/http-api.md) says the same of `POST /api/anki` (#233).
* **Update**: [Saving](/reference/editor-keys.md#saving) holds in two cases it missed. A write kasten took as its own, a bookmark's or the autosave's, no longer outlives a switch to another note, so coming back and typing then meeting an outside write of that same text shows `Changed on disk` (#251). Served without `crypto.subtle`, over plain http, such a text is no longer trusted for good, so the same outside write is a conflict there too (#252).
* **Update**: [Import an Anki deck](/how-to/import-an-anki-deck.md) gained a section on formatting: a field's bold, italic, underline, links, lists, code and ruby arrive as markdown, its line breaks as lines of a side written in the `?` form, and the furigana filters as Anki applies them (#234). A line that would end a card is now kept with a backslash rather than left out, and "What does not survive" no longer says a field is flattened to one line.
* **Correction**: the review overview counts cards with `parseCards`, the parser the sitting asks them with, and no longer off the lines the scan matched. A `::` or cloze line under a `?` back read as a second card on the overview and as part of the back in the sitting (#257). The cost is one note read per deck note where there were none.
* **Correction**: a flush on leaving a note that fails no longer writes that note's text into the next one on its next save. The bar says `Not saved` and names the note that lost its text instead, with no note open too (#256). [Use kasten on a phone](/how-to/use-kasten-on-a-phone.md) and [the editor keys](/reference/editor-keys.md) name the new state.
* **Correction**: [Saving](/reference/editor-keys.md#saving) loses no text on the way out. Hiding the page (a reload, a closed tab, a phone sending the app to the background) writes waiting text at once, where text typed in the last 800 ms used to go with the page (#261). A failed write of a note left behind, or of the note open as you leave the editor for another screen, keeps that note's text in browser storage until it is written or dropped; `Not saved: <note>` stays in the bar through saves elsewhere, and opening the note puts the text back under `Could not save` (#259). A failed write offers `Drop my text` beside `Write my text`. [Use kasten on a phone](/how-to/use-kasten-on-a-phone.md) says the same.
* **Update**: [Import an Anki deck](/how-to/import-an-anki-deck.md) gained "Import a newer version of a deck": each imported card ends in `<!--anki:NID:ORD:HASH-->`, and a re-import merges into the deck's note by it, adding new cards, rewriting cards Anki changed with their kasten schedule and parking kept, keeping the rest untouched, a card edited in kasten among them, and leaving cards deleted in Anki in the note, counted as removed. A deck imported before the comment existed is still refused. **Carry Anki's schedule**, off by default, writes Anki's due date, interval and ease onto every added card Anki has in review. [HTTP API](/reference/http-api.md#post-apianki) says the same, names `?carry=true` and the `added`, `updated`, `kept` and `removed` counts, and [Flashcard format](/reference/flashcard-format.md#what-is-not-read) says the review never shows the comment (#235).
* **Correction**: [Saving](/reference/editor-keys.md#saving) also holds the text of an autosave still on its way when the page hides, and sends it again the way it sends waiting text. Before, a reload during that round trip could cancel the write and lose the text (#264).
* **Correction**: [Saving](/reference/editor-keys.md#saving) no longer writes a note that reads `Changed on disk` when you leave it for another note, click into another pane or leave the editor; before, that wrote your text over the other writer's. kasten keeps your text in browser storage and writes nothing, the bar reads `Changed on disk: <note>`, and opening the note shows the conflict again. A conflict opens a diff of the vault's text against yours above the bar by itself. [Use kasten on a phone](/how-to/use-kasten-on-a-phone.md) says the same (#268). Moving a note or its folder in kasten carries text kept for it, a failed save's or a conflict's, to the new path (#271, in part).
