* **Update**: [HTTP API](/reference/http-api.md) gained `PATCH /api/folders/{path}`, which gives a folder a new path and takes every note under it along. The section says why a folder needs a route of its own rather than the note's, why the move is one rename rather than a walk over the subtree, and why merging into a folder that already exists is refused.
* **Update**: [Editor keys](/reference/editor-keys.md) gained the tree's `c` and `r`, the first bare letters on the page that are not navigation. `<leader>rf` now says the `f` names a file and that `r` is the key for a row of either kind. "The note prompt" covers the third mode, `rename folder`, which adds no `.md`, leaves the folder's own subtree out of the completion list, and says how many notes the move carries.
* **Update**: [Getting started](/tutorials/getting-started.md) gained a step that moves a whole folder and watches the vault refuse a folder inside itself and a folder onto one that exists. Its "what you cannot do yet" section no longer says folders are renamed by hand, and says instead that a folder is never made on its own.
* **Creation**: Added [The note finder](/reference/editor-keys.md#the-note-finder) to Editor keys, reached by `<leader>ff` and the tree's `f`. The section says what the list ranks, what the pane beside it shows, and why Tab is unbound there: the finder is the other way round from the prompt, the list being the answer and the input only a filter.
* **Rename**: [Note prompt performance](/reference/ranking-performance.md) became [Ranking performance](/reference/ranking-performance.md), one scorer now serving the prompt and the finder both. Every recorded number was measured again, and every threshold with them: ranking the folders a keystroke pays for fell from 0.372ms to 0.092ms, and the prompt's keystroke in Chromium from 5.1ms to 2.5ms. The page gained the finder's own numbers, 6.7ms for a keystroke at 10,000 notes and about 21ms at 50,000, and says why ranking notes with the old scorer would have cost 156ms.
* **Update**: [HTTP API](/reference/http-api.md) gained `GET /api/search`, which finds every line in the vault holding a query. The section says why nothing is indexed, why the match is literal rather than fuzzy, which flags keep it seeing exactly what `GET /api/files` lists, and why a blank query answers with nothing rather than everything.
